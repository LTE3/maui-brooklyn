01   About

An island in Brooklyn.

Two oceans, one room. Maui Brooklyn sits on a quiet corner of Myrtle Avenue, where Caribbean spice meets Hawaiian fire. Plates travel from the smokehouse to the bar without losing the warmth that started them.

The cocktail list leans tequila-forward and rum-proud — built around a Piña Colada that we will defend with our lives. The kitchen runs on charcoal, coconut, jerk, and patience. The room runs on conversation.
