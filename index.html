Maui has docked.

Welcome to Maui at the Marina: a limited-time summer waterfront pop-up bringing the spirit of the islands to 163-45 Cross Bay Blvd, Jamaica, NY 11414.

Escape the city
without leaving it.

Arrive by land or by water. Dock your own boat and spend the day, or rent a jet ski and explore the bay before coming back to eat, drink, and unwind. Brunch, dinner, or a full day on the water: Maui is your summer destination.

The layout.

Two levels on the water: rooftop lounge with the screen and grill, dockside dining with the Maui Bar and jet skis.
