Concept

Two islands, one block.

Caribbean kitchen meets Hawaiian tiki on Myrtle Ave. Built for long Friday nights and lazy Sunday afternoons.

Maui Brooklyn is a love letter to two oceans. We pull recipes from Trinidad, Jamaica, Puerto Rico, Oahu, and the Big Island. Then we plate them where the L train ends.

Tiki classics rebuilt with fresh juice and aged rum. A short menu of plates designed to share. Sound system tuned for Burning Spear at midnight and Don Ho on Sunday brunch.

No pretense. No frozen mixers. Just food, drinks, and a back patio that feels three thousand miles from the BQE.
