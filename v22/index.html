Our Story

Painted in blue, poured in rum.

Maui Brooklyn began as a postcard between two cooks — one raised on Oahu coconut palms, the other on Trinidadian pepper sauce — and the question they kept asking: what if a corner bar in Ridgewood smelled like a beach kitchen? The tiles you see on every wall came hand-painted from a small workshop outside Puebla, the rums from across the wider Caribbean, and the recipes from a lifetime of cooking for the people we love.

We are not a theme. We are a Sunday-afternoon kitchen with a long bar and the doors wide open. Sit down, share a Painkiller, eat with your hands, stay until the lanterns get switched on. The trade winds, we like to think, eventually reach Myrtle Avenue.

— Kainoa & Davi, your hosts
