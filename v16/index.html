// THE STORY

An island bar that landed in Brooklyn.

Maui Brooklyn is a tropical love letter pinned to a New York wall. Born from late-night beach memories and Williamsburg humidity, our kitchen welds Caribbean spice to Hawaiian sunshine — jerk-rubbed pork bowls, smoked-pineapple poke, hibiscus-glazed wings, and a rum cabinet that takes itself just seriously enough.

The room is loud on purpose. Bamboo, terrazzo, neon palms, vintage Sottsass-shaped lamps and a soundtrack that swings from Calypso to Hawaiian slack-key to early MTV. Come for a Mai Tai. Stay for the second one. Tell your boss you got stuck on a flight from Honolulu.
