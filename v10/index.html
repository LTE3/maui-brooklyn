[ Chapter 01 ] — The Story

Two Islands.
One Block.

Maui Brooklyn is a love letter folded in banana leaf — where the smoke of Trinidadian jerk meets the sweetness of Hawaiian luau, and the bass of a Bushwick block party keeps the time. Born on Myrtle Ave, our kitchen riffs on the dishes our grandmothers shouted across kitchens from Kingston to Honolulu — and plates them up in a room loud enough to remind you you're alive.

Behind the bar, our tiki team is rebuilding the cocktail canon for a new generation. Aged rums, fresh-pressed pineapple, charred lime, Scotch bonnet, and a stubborn refusal to use anything from a plastic bottle. Come for the Mai Tai. Stay for the conch fritters. Leave humming a song you didn't know you knew.
