La Maison
— a tropical interlude —

Brooklyn, NY

Maui Brooklyn is a small ceremony performed every evening on Myrtle Avenue — a kitchen and bar where the Caribbean meets Hawaii under low lamplight. The room is tropical without nostalgia, refined without restraint: dark wood, ochre tile, palm shadows that shift with the ceiling fan, and a back-bar lined with rum and rhum agricole, mezcal and tequila, dark sugar and bright citrus.

The menu is a quiet correspondence between two archipelagos. Pineapple from Maui, lime from Brooklyn's market shelves, smoke from a grill that never quite goes cold. We pour the classics with respect — the Mai Tai, the Painkiller, the Pina Colada — and tilt them, gently, toward the city outside the window. Come early for the light. Stay late for the rum.
