Origin Story

A garden, after dark.

Maui Brooklyn is a Caribbean & Hawaiian tiki bar living inside a converted plant conservatory off Myrtle Ave. By day the room is a sun-soaked greenhouse — banana leaves, monstera, weeping ferns. After sundown the windows fog, the neon flickers on, and the room turns into the cocktail bar your favorite uncle from Oahu would have opened in 1974, if he ever made it to Bushwick.

Our pour is aged rum, hand-cut citrus, and house-clarified pineapple. The kitchen is jerk-spiced, kalua-smoked, and built to be eaten with your hands while a Bobby Womack record plays at 78 decibels. It's loud. It's warm. The plants are real. You're staying for one more.

House Philosophy

Aged rum, hand-cut citrus, no shortcuts —
a 1974 tiki room rebuilt by a 2026 Brooklyn band.

We clarify our own pineapple. We toast our own orgeat almonds. The fern in the corner has a name (her name is Linda). Come early for the kitchen, come late for the band, come Wednesday for the secret rum list.
