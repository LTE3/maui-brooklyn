Maui Brooklyn is what happens when a St. Croix kitchen and a Honolulu bar end up sharing a Bushwick walk-up. We pour drinks like our grandmothers cooked — heavy on the rum, easy on the rules. The room is small, the speakers are loud, and the pineapple is always cold. You're in Brooklyn, but for ninety minutes you're not.

Every cocktail on this side is hand-batched and hand-labeled. We squeeze our own lime, toast our own orgeat, crush mint by hand. There is no Side B for the kitchen — only for the bar — but we'll grill jerk wings and snapper until close. No skips, no filler. Just a long warm humid mixtape with the corner of the cassette inlay slightly bent from being passed around too much.
