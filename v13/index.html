≋ Aloha + Yo ≋

Two Islands
One Bar

Maui Brooklyn is a tiki kitchen and rum bar where the trade winds of the Caribbean crash into the volcanic shores of the Pacific — and both wash up on Myrtle Avenue. Conceived as a love letter to the parrot-shirt cocktail dens of the 1960s and the corner spots of Bed-Stuy, we make drinks the long way: fresh juices squeezed in-house, demerara syrup boiled down each morning, and aged rums from Barbados, Jamaica, Martinique, Trinidad, and a few we won't name on a website.

The kitchen riffs equally on jerk smoke, kalua pork, escovitch fire, lomi-lomi snap and Brooklyn diner instinct. The room is black-lit, ferny, fully shellacked. There is a velvet painting. There is a fog machine. The Mai Tais are large. Pull up a rattan stool and stay a while — outside it's still winter, but in here it's permanently August.
