// 01 — ABOUT

A Punk Tiki
Love Letter

Read it twice.

Maui Brooklyn is what happens when a Caribbean grandma's rum cabinet runs into a Hawaiian aunty's pineapple knife on a hot night in Ridgewood. We pour stiff, we plate loud, and the playlist is mostly questionable. Our kitchen runs on smoke and citrus — jerk, kalua, ginger, scotch bonnet, lime — and our bar believes the tiki drink is a 4-minute vacation you sip through a paper straw.

This isn't a corporate concept. This is a 28-seat room with mismatched stools, a velvet shark on the wall, and a bartender named Junior who will absolutely judge your order and then make it better. We're at 66-48 Myrtle Ave, the door is painted teal, the lights are pink. You can't miss us. Don't.

— xoxo, the staff
