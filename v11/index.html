Salt, smoke, and shellfish.

Long tables on the dock. Boats close enough to touch. The room fills when the sun drops.

Same family. Different water.

Maui Brooklyn put the islands on Myrtle Ave. Swan Marina puts them back on the water.

The Brooklyn room was always landlocked. Good rum, good fire, no horizon. We wanted the other half of it: a dock, a hull line, gulls arguing over the ice.

So the second room sits at Swan Marina in Queens. The raw bar runs the length of it. The fish comes in whole and leaves on a plate the same day, and the rum list stays exactly as long as it needs to be.

Nothing here is precious. Paper on the tables, shells in the bucket, your hands are going to smell like lime until Tuesday.
