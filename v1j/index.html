The Concept

Two Islands. One Table.

Maui Brooklyn brings together the smoky, citrus-forward soul of the Caribbean and the sweet, savory richness of Hawaii under one tropical roof. Think jerk-glazed kalua pork, plantain poke, and rum-laced tiki cocktails poured next to ice-cold Hawaiian lagers.

Every plate is built for sharing. Every drink is built for the long, slow sunset that never actually sets in Brooklyn.

What We're About

Aloha meets Irie.

🌺

Caribbean-Hawaiian Kitchen

Bold island flavor that blends jerk, curry, and citrus with kalua, poke, and pineapple. Built for sharing.

🍹

Rum-Forward Tiki Bar

A serious rum program, fresh-pressed juices, and tiki classics done right. Mai Tais, painkillers, planters punch.

🔥

Late-Night Energy

Dinner gives way to DJ nights, live percussion, and weekend programming that runs deep into the morning.

🌴

Made for Brooklyn

Designed for the borough that demands better food, bigger drinks, and a room you actually want to stay in.
