— our story —

a little Aloha in Ridgewood

Tucked into a sun-washed corner of Myrtle Avenue, Maui Brooklyn is what happens when a Hawaiian surf shack falls in love with a Caribbean rum bar. We hand-lettered the menu, salvaged the driftwood, and tied everything together with a length of marina rope. The result is a kitchen and bar that feels less like a restaurant and more like a long weekend at someone’s beach house.

Our cocktails lean into the slow side of life—orgeat we toast in-house, pineapple we juice every morning, and a backbar stacked with funky Jamaican rums, blanco tequilas, and one really good vodka. The kitchen pulls from Oahu plate lunches and Trinidadian doubles in equal measure. Come hungry. Stay late. Borrow the ukulele.
