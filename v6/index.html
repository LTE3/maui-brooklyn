// the signal

Tropical, Tuned Up.

Two islands — one frequency. Maui Brooklyn is a Caribbean & Hawaiian kitchen and tiki bar broadcasting from Myrtle Ave on a 1985 carrier wave.

We fly Kalua pig past Kingston, drag oxtail through Honolulu, and finish every plate under a neon palm. The cocktails arrive in chrome. The kitchen runs hot. The grid never quits.

Open late. Lit pink. Built for nights you'll lie about later.
