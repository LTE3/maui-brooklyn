PAGE 02 — THE STORY

Two islands, one kitchen.

We grew up between two coasts — Sundays at Trini doubles carts in Crown Heights, summers digging kalua pig out of an imu in Hana. When we moved to Bushwick and couldn't find a single place serving both jerk and poke, we figured: cut it out, tape it together, make a zine of a restaurant.

Maui Brooklyn is what happened. Real Caribbean stews from Auntie's notebook, real Hawaiian plates from Tutu's kitchen, served on enamel plates under fishnet ceilings in a converted bodega on Myrtle Ave. The cocktails are old-school tiki with Brooklyn brattitude.

"It's not a fusion. It's a friendship."

Family run. Cash & cards. No dress code unless your shirt is dry — then it's not a tiki bar, it's a brunch.
