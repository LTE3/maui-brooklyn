FROM THE COUNTER

Two islands. One corner store.

A scrappy little bodega kitchen run by cooks from Port-au-Prince, Kingston, Honolulu and Bed-Stuy — pulling shifts together, painting signs together, feeding the block.

The Caribbean Side

Slow-cooked oxtail, jerk-rubbed snapper, rice and peas the way Tante used to make it on Sunday. Scotch bonnet heat, allspice smoke, plantains crackling in the back. The flavors that ride the J train.

The Hawaiian Side

Kalua pig steamed til it falls, ahi poke layered with crisp veg, huli-huli chicken kissed by the grill. Aloha through and through, plated on enamel diner ware. Tropical heart, Brooklyn hustle.
