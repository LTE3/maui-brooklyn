01 — The house

Salt air, neon dusk, two oceans on one bar.

Maui Brooklyn is a love letter folded between two coastlines — the slow-burn island swing of the Caribbean and the volcanic sweetness of the Hawaiian Pacific — squeezed into a corner of Ridgewood that already smelled a little like rum. We pour from a deep bench of aged and agricole rums, throw a lot of fresh fruit around, and keep the music warm enough to forget what month it is.

The kitchen runs jerk, kalua, escovitch and poke — small, sharp, made for sharing across a low table. The bar runs everything else. There is no dress code, but there is a dress code: come like you're already on vacation. Tiki mugs are encouraged, shoes are required, and the painkillers, somehow, actually work.
