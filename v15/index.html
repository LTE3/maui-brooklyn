Prologue · A Note from the Compilers

An illustrated compendium of two warm coasts.

Maui Brooklyn began as a private journal of voyages — pages stained with rum, lime, and the salt of two oceans. We gathered the cooking fires of the Caribbean and the smoke of Hawaiian luaus, then transplanted them, like cuttings under glass, to a tiled corner of Ridgewood-adjacent Myrtle Avenue.

What you will find within is part bar, part kitchen, part field guide. Every cocktail is plated like a specimen; every dish traces a route from Kingston or Kahului through Brooklyn's iron-clad bridges. Sit beneath the palms. Order generously. Linger long enough to read the captions.

Marginalia. The proprietors keep an open ledger of song requests and small island stories. Add yours to the leather book by the host stand.
